# Consumer Price Index (CPI) Analysis of India

## Project Overview

This project analyzes India's Consumer Price Index (CPI) data using Microsoft Excel and Google Sheets to understand inflation trends, category-wise price movements, food inflation, and changes in consumer prices over time.

The analysis covers Rural, Urban, and Rural+Urban sectors and examines major CPI categories and food categories.

## Objectives

- Analyze CPI and inflation trends over time
- Compare CPI movements across major categories
- Analyze Year-over-Year (YoY) inflation
- Analyze food inflation trends
- Identify broad category contributions
- Analyze food category contributions
- Examine CPI movements during the COVID-19 period
- Compare Rural, Urban, and Rural+Urban trends
- Study the relationship between crude oil prices and CPI categories using correlation analysis
- Present findings using tables, charts, and visualizations

## Analysis Performed

### 1. CPI Trend Analysis

Analyzed CPI movements over time to identify changes in consumer prices across different sectors and categories.

### 2. YoY Inflation Analysis

Analyzed Year-over-Year inflation trends to understand changes in prices compared with the corresponding period of the previous year.

### 3. Food Inflation Analysis

Examined food inflation trends and changes across individual food categories.

### 4. Broad Category Contribution

Compared major CPI categories to understand their contribution and relationship with overall CPI movements.

### 5. Food Category Contribution

Analyzed individual food categories to identify categories with stronger price movements and contributions.

### 6. COVID-19 Impact Analysis

Examined CPI trends around the COVID-19 period to identify changes in inflation and category-level movements.

### 7. Correlation Analysis

Performed correlation analysis to study the relationship between crude oil price changes and CPI categories. The analysis evaluates both the direction and strength of the relationships.
